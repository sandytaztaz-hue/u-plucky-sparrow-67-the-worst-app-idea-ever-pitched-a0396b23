One sentence pitching the worst, most useless app imaginable: PocketCheck, a
$3-a-month app that texts your phone every morning to confirm your phone is still
in your pocket — and if it cannot reach your phone, concludes you have lost your
pocket. A perfect mismatch between a problem nobody has and the app that solves
it, delivered with the deadpan confidence of a founder who means every word.
The uselessness is the product: the phone confirms the phone, and the failure
mode blames the pocket.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program writes the pitch to `/output/entry.txt`.
